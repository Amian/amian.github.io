Basic Privacy Policy (No Data Collected)

Last Updated: September 2025

This policy applies to apps that do not collect, store, or share any personal data. It is intended as a simple, generic statement for apps whose functionality does not require user data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information or usage data from users. The app operates entirely on-device and does not transmit user data to servers.

2. Internet, APIs, and Third-Party Services

The app does not use external APIs, analytics, or third-party services that collect data. No data is sent to external servers for processing.

3. Tracking and Advertising

The app does not include advertising, does not track users, and does not use cookies, device identifiers, or any tracking technologies.

4. Data Storage

No user data is stored on our servers or within the app beyond what is required for immediate functionality during active use. When you close the app, no personal information is retained.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any data, it does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe something in the app could collect data unintentionally, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the app’s functionality changes in a way that involves data collection, this policy will be updated before any such changes take effect.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@anummian.com.

We respect your privacy. This app is designed to work without collecting personal data.
