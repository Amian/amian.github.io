Privacy Policy

Identifier apps with a Community feed — by Mian Ltd

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Overview

This policy applies to our identifier apps that include the optional Community feed (for example Bird Identifier, Fish Identifier and similar apps that show a "Community" tab). Our apps identify things you photograph and, if you choose, let you share those identifications with other users of the same app.

Apps of ours without a Community tab are covered by our standard policy instead.

What we collect and why

  • Photos you scan. Sent to our identification service to produce the result, and stored on your device. A scanned photo is only published if you explicitly post it to the Community feed.
  • Community account. When you first use the Community feed we create an anonymous account for you automatically (a random user ID and a generated display name — no email, password or real name required). You can optionally link Sign in with Apple or Google so your profile survives reinstalls; if you do, our authentication provider (Google Firebase) stores the email address associated with that sign-in.
  • Content you post. Posts (photo, identification details, caption), comments, your display name, and any profile photo or bio you set are publicly visible to other users of the same app. Post images are stored on Cloudflare R2 (our image host); profile and post text is stored in Google Firebase.
  • Crash and performance data, device identifiers. Collected via Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics) to keep the app working and understand aggregate usage.

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or share your data with third parties beyond the processors listed below.

Processors we use

  • Google Firebase (authentication, community database, crash reporting, analytics)
  • Cloudflare R2 (hosting of community images)
  • Google Gemini API (image identification, including an automated safety check that flags photos unsuitable for public posting)
  • RevenueCat / Apple / Google (subscription processing — we never see your payment details)

Retention & deletion

  • Community content stays published until you delete it or delete your account.
  • You can delete individual posts and comments in the app at any time.
  • Account deletion is available in the app (Community profile → Delete Account). It permanently removes your profile, your posts, your comments and your profile photo. See our account deletion page for details and a web request option.
  • Images belonging to deleted posts are removed from public access and purged from storage on a scheduled cleanup.
  • Scan history that you never post never leaves your device except for the identification request itself.

Moderation

Community content is user-generated. We provide in-app reporting (posts, comments and users), blocking, automatic hiding of repeatedly-reported content, and we review reports daily. See the Community Guidelines & Terms.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and the Community feed requires the app's minimum age per its store listing.

Your rights & contact

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data. The in-app account deletion covers the common case; for anything else contact us at contact@theapptor.com and we will respond within 30 days.
