Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2025

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share information about you through our iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

App Data: The app does not collect or store personal data for profiling or analytics. All core interactions with the app are conducted without requiring user accounts.

Purchase Data (RevenueCat): To support in‑app purchases and subscriptions, we use RevenueCat. As part of processing purchases, RevenueCat may receive information such as purchase receipts, a device or app‑scoped identifier, country/region, and IP address for fraud prevention and entitlement management. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy for details.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

App Functionality: The app works without collecting personal data for personalization or analytics.

Purchases: Any data handled by RevenueCat is used solely to validate purchases, manage subscription entitlements, provide access to paid features, and prevent fraud.

3. How We Use Your Data

a. Storage: We do not store user profile data on our own servers.

b. Purchases: Purchase and subscription events are processed via RevenueCat. We use the entitlement status provided by RevenueCat to unlock premium features within the app.

4. Data Sharing

App Data: We do not share personal information with third parties, as we do not collect it.

Purchase Processing: Purchase‑related data is processed by RevenueCat as our service provider to enable subscriptions and entitlements. We do not sell user data.

5. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage in‑app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat’s collection and use of data are governed by their Privacy Policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that any data has been collected inadvertently, we will take steps to delete such information from our records as soon as possible.

7. Your Choices

Subscriptions: You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your app store account settings (e.g., Apple App Store or Google Play).

Privacy Controls: You can adjust device privacy settings (network access, permissions) if you prefer to limit any connectivity used for purchase validation.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new policy on our website and update the effective date. We encourage you to review our Privacy Policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@anummian.com

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure and enjoyable user experience.
