Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2025

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share information about you through our iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

App Data: The app does not collect or store any personal data from its users. All interactions with the app are conducted anonymously.

Advertising Data (if applicable): If the app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, Google may collect certain information for advertising purposes. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • App usage data
  • Location data (if permitted)

This advertising data collection is handled by Google AdMob and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

App Functionality: Since we do not collect personal data, the app's functionality is designed to be used without the need for user data storage or personalization.

Advertising (if applicable): If the app displays ads, Google AdMob may use collected data to:

  • Show relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Improve ad targeting
  • Prevent fraud

3. How We Use Your Data

a. Storage: Since we do not collect personal data, there is no storage of user profile information.

b. Advertising: We do not directly use advertising data. Any advertising-related data collection and usage is handled by Google AdMob according to their privacy practices.

4. Data Sharing

App Data: We do not share any personal information with third parties, as we do not collect any. Information processed for identification is not shared or stored.

Advertising Data: If the app displays ads, Google AdMob may share advertising data with their advertising partners and service providers as outlined in their Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob: If the app displays advertisements, it uses Google AdMob. Google's collection and use of data is governed by their Privacy Policy, which can be found at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that any data has been collected inadvertently, we will take steps to delete such information from our records as soon as possible.

If the app displays ads, Google AdMob complies with applicable children's privacy laws and regulations.

7. Your Choices

Advertising: If the app displays ads, you can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's advertising settings or by visiting Google's Ad Settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Data Collection: You can limit data collection by adjusting your device's privacy settings and permissions.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new policy on our website and update the effective date. We encourage you to review our Privacy Policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@anummian.com

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure and enjoyable user experience.
