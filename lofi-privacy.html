Privacy Policy

Lofi: beats to breathe to

Last updated: July 13, 2026

The short version

Lofi is a music player. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or microphone. All music plays from files bundled inside the app. We collect only anonymous usage statistics, crash reports, and purchase state so the app works and gets better.

What we collect

Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). Anonymous events such as which stations and tracks are played, which screens are viewed, and which features (favorites, sleep timer) are used, along with a device identifier, device model, OS version, and country. This tells us which parts of the app people enjoy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a technical report (stack trace, device model, OS version) is sent so we can fix the bug. It contains no personal information.

Purchase state (RevenueCat). If you subscribe or buy the lifetime unlock, an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt are processed by RevenueCat to activate premium on your device. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't collect names, emails, or contact details. We don't access your location, contacts, photos, files, or microphone. We don't show third-party ads. We don't sell or share your data with data brokers. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data retention & your choices

Analytics and crash data are retained by Google Firebase under their standard retention windows and are not tied to your identity. Purchase records persist so your premium unlock keeps working (and can be restored on a new device via "Restore purchases"). Deleting the app removes all locally stored preferences, including favorites.

To request deletion of the anonymous analytics or purchase records associated with your device, email us and we'll handle it.

Third-party services

The services above operate under their own privacy policies: Google (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics) · RevenueCat · Apple · Google Play.

Children

Lofi is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the update will appear on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: contact@theapptor.com.
