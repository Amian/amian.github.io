Delete Your Community Account

Identifier apps by Mian Ltd (Bird Identifier, Fish Identifier, and similar apps with a Community tab)

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Delete in the app (fastest — immediate)

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Community profile (or tap your avatar in the Community tab).
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile (display name, photo, bio), all your posts, all your comments, and your sign-in record. If you linked Sign in with Apple, the Apple authorisation is revoked too. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email (if you can't access the app)

Email contact@theapptor.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Include the app name and your Community display name (and, if you linked one, the Apple/Google email you signed in with — accounts are otherwise anonymous, so the display name is how we find yours). We verify and complete email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what isn't

  • Deleted: your profile, your posts (including their images from public access), your comments, your profile photo, your sign-in/auth record.
  • Not tied to your account: your on-device scan history (delete the app or its data to remove it) and aggregate, non-identifying analytics.
  • Like counts on other people's posts are anonymous tallies and don't identify you after deletion.
