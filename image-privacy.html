Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2024

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and handle information in our image generation iOS app.

1. Information We Process

Our app processes images that you upload for the purpose of generating new images using AI technology. This may include images containing faces. We do not collect or store any personal data from our users, including face data. All image processing is conducted in real-time and images are not permanently stored on our servers.

2. Face Data Handling

a. Collection: Our app may process images containing faces as part of the image generation process. However, we do not specifically collect, extract, or store face data separately from the images you upload.

b. Usage: Any face data present in uploaded images is processed solely for the purpose of generating new images. The face data is processed in real-time and is not used for any other purpose, including but not limited to facial recognition, identification, or tracking.

c. Third-Party Sharing: Face data is not shared with any third parties. When images containing faces are processed through our AI partners (OpenAI or Google Gemini), the data is transmitted securely and processed according to their respective privacy policies. The face data is not stored by these third parties after processing is complete.

d. Storage and Retention: Face data is not stored on our servers. Images containing faces are processed in real-time and are automatically deleted after processing is complete. The only storage of images containing faces occurs locally on your device if you choose to save the generated images.

3. Image Processing and AI Technology

When you upload an image to our app:

  • The image is temporarily processed using AI models (OpenAI or Google Gemini)
  • The original image is not stored permanently on our servers
  • The generated output image is provided to you immediately
  • All processing is done in real-time and images are deleted after processing

4. How We Handle Your Images

a. Processing: Images are processed using AI models to generate new images based on your input. This processing is done in real-time and is necessary for the core functionality of the app.

b. Storage: We do not permanently store any images on our servers. Images are temporarily held in memory only during the processing period and are automatically deleted afterward.

c. Local Storage: Any images you choose to save are stored locally on your device only, and we have no access to these saved images.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services for image processing:

  • OpenAI's API for image generation
  • Google Gemini's API for image generation

These services process images according to their respective privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your images during the brief processing period. All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or process any information from children under 13. If we learn that any data has been collected inadvertently, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new policy on our website and update the effective date. We encourage you to review our Privacy Policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our image processing practices, please contact us at contact@anummian.com

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure and enjoyable user experience.
